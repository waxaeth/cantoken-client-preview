CanToken Offerings

This page will host information about private placements structured by issuers and tokenized using CanToken’s technology. Each offering will include a high-level summary with links to official documents provided by the issuer or its exempt market dealer, where applicable.

CanToken itself does not create, sell or promote securities. We provide technology rails for issuers and their registered intermediaries. They use these workflows to represent ownership interests in digital token form, maintain records and manage transfers over time.

How a tokenized private placement works

Each offering follows four steps:

  1. The issuer and its legal or registrant team structure the deal under existing securities laws, using the applicable exemptions.
  2. The economic and legal terms are encoded into security tokens with on-chain rules, such as eligibility, holding periods and transfer restrictions, using CanToken’s technology.
  3. A registered dealer or exempt market dealer, where involved, handles investor onboarding, suitability and subscription processing.
  4. A transfer agent or registrar maintains the official register, which is synchronized with the tokens.

The goal is to preserve the same rights, restrictions and protections as a traditional private placement. Records, monitoring and administration are handled digitally over the life of the investment.

What is distributed ledger technology?

Distributed ledger technology (DLT) is a shared record of transactions kept across many computers, so no single party can change it alone.

Regulatory status of tokenized securities

Tokenized securities are generally traditional securities represented in digital form and remain subject to the same securities laws, exemptions, and regulatory oversight as non-tokenized securities. The digital format and record-keeping method do not change whether an instrument is a security.
