About CanToken

About CanToken: governed asset tokenization in Canada

CanToken follows a set order: the asset first, its legal structure second, the token last.

Mission

CanToken’s differentiator is not tokenization technology. It is governed sequencing.

Real assets first

CanToken starts with the real-world asset and the underlying deal before any technology is applied.

Legal and governance containers second

We then structure those assets inside clear legal, regulatory and governance frameworks so rights, obligations and decision-making are well-defined and enforceable.

Tokenization last

Only after the asset and its legal container are solid do we tokenize, using security tokens on distributed ledger technology (DLT) to support digital records and transfer rules.

Vision

CanToken’s vision is to efficiently bring real Canadian private market assets to the world, to structure ongoing generosity into every product, and to participate in a broader national story of faithful stewardship and economic revival.

Shared value

CanToken commits 10% of its pre-tax revenue to vetted nonprofit organizations.

We see building markets as carrying a responsibility to the communities around them. Giving back is part of how we operate.

We invite other companies in our industry to make their own commitment, whether through time, expertise or a share of revenue. Service takes many forms.

Who we work with

Polymath provides tokenization infrastructure for on-chain securities. CanToken provides technology and marketing; any activity requiring registration is handled by the applicable licensed parties.
