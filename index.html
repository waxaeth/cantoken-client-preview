Contact / FAQ

Are you a dealer?+

No. CanToken provides technology and marketing; registrable activity is performed by licensed intermediaries.

Who can invest in private placements in Canada?+

Private placements in Canada may be open to accredited, eligible or self-certified investors, and to close friends, family or business associates of the issuer. Eligibility rules vary by province.

A registered dealer or exempt market dealer handles applicable onboarding, KYC/AML and suitability.

How are tokenized securities regulated?+

Tokenized securities are generally traditional securities represented in digital form and remain subject to the same securities laws, exemptions, and regulatory oversight as non-tokenized securities. The digital format and record-keeping method do not change whether an instrument is a security.

Can non-Canadian issuers or investors use CanToken?+

Tokenized private placements in Canadian and cross-border deals, focused on real assets and high-governance issuers.

Any investment decision must be made solely on the basis of the official offering documents provided by the issuer and their registered dealer, and only by persons who meet applicable eligibility requirements.
