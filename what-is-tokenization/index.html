Understanding tokenized securities

What is tokenization? Tokenized securities in Canada

Tokenization records rights in a real asset digitally. Securities laws, eligibility requirements and investor rights still apply.

What CanToken is not

Not an anonymous scheme

Issuers, investors and intermediaries are identified through KYC/AML onboarding.

Not a way around tax rules

Using digital records does not remove legal or tax obligations.

Not a speculative coin

A security token is a security recorded on a blockchain, with an issuer, legal rights and disclosure behind it.

Not outside oversight

Tokenized securities remain subject to the Canadian securities rules that apply to the underlying investment.

How CanToken uses tokenization

What is real-world asset (RWA) tokenization?

Real-world asset (RWA) tokenization turns ownership rights in a physical or financial asset into digital tokens on a blockchain built for securities, so they can be divided, tracked and transferred digitally. A tokenized security is a traditional security in digital form, under the same rules.

CanToken gives conservative market participants a way to use tokenization, from efficiency to programmability, without abandoning the safeguards that make capital formation sustainable in the first place.

Why tokenize a private placement?

Private placements, especially in exploration and other SMEs, sit in long lockups with little or no secondary market. Tokenization is designed to address those structural frictions: high minimums, manual transfers and limited venues.

By breaking ownership into standardized, programmable units, tokenization can support fractional positions and digital settlement. Exemptions and holding periods still apply.

Is tokenization legal in Canada?

Tokenizing a security does not change the rules that apply to it. Custody, disclosure, KYC/AML and investor rights rules stay in force.

CanToken builds rails that work within existing exempt frameworks. Tools for issuers or exempt market dealers include digital cap tables, automated transfer restrictions and audit trails.

Where capital markets are heading

Real-world asset tokenization is moving from pilot projects into wider use. In a 2026 Apex Group survey of 100 asset management executives, half said their firms had introduced tokenization in some form.

CanToken gives companies, exempt market dealers and investors a way to take part in that shift. Its technology is designed for prudence, transparency and long-term trust.

What tokenized securities mean for investors

By tokenizing interests under existing securities rules, CanToken aims to give you digital records and programmable transfer restrictions. Custody, KYC/AML and rights enforcement stay in place. Resale remains subject to hold periods under securities law.

What tokenization means for private placement issuers

CanToken gives you digital tools for managing private placements, within the regulatory frameworks your counsel already understands. Tokenizing your existing exempt offerings can automate cap table management and transfer restrictions.
