How tokenized offerings work on CanToken

The process behind tokenized private placements, from legal structure to the official register.

CanToken1 min read

On this page

  1. How tokenized offerings work on CanToken

This page will host information about private placements that have been structured and tokenized using CanToken’s infrastructure. Each offering will include a high-level summary and links to official documents provided by the issuer and their exempt market dealer or registered dealer, where applicable.

How tokenized offerings work on CanToken

  1. The issuer and their legal/registrant team structure the deal under existing securities laws and exemptions.

  2. The economic and legal terms are encoded into tokens and associated on-chain rules (e.g., eligibility, holding periods, transfer restrictions) using CanToken’s infrastructure.

  3. A registered dealer or exempt market dealer handles investor onboarding, KYC/KYB, AML and suitability as applicable.

  4. A transfer agent and/or registrar maintains the official register, which is synchronized with the tokenized representation.

The goal is to preserve the same rights, restrictions, and protections as a traditional private placement—while improving how they are recorded, monitored, and administered over the life of the investment.
